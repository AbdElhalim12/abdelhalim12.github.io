Material Replacer

How To Use

- Add The Package to your project.

- go to the tools menu

- click on Material Replacer tool

in the panel there is a place to add the prefab.

Note: If you add a prefab from the scene you will be able to undo the changes. If you added it from project folder you will not be able to undo the changes. So be careful.

 

there is 2 columns for the materials

- the left one is the materials that already exists on the GameObjects in your prefab.

- the right column is the new material that you want to replace.

 

there is 3 buttons:

- Clear All Materials: will clear the materials fields in case you miss put a material.

- Replace Materials: after you put your new materials you can click on this button to replace them.

- Restore Original Materials: will revert to the original material of the GameObjects.
